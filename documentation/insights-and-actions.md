# Insights and Leadership Actions

## West Midlands: highest volume and highest relative burden

Recorded crime totals:

| Force | Approx. total recorded crime |
|---|---:|
| West Midlands | **933k** |
| Staffordshire | **263k** |
| West Mercia | **257k** |
| Warwickshire | **122k** |

Average monthly crime rate per 1,000 residents:

| Force | Rate |
|---|---:|
| West Midlands | **8.5** |
| Staffordshire | **6.2** |
| West Mercia | **5.3** |
| Warwickshire | **5.3** |

### Leadership action

Prioritise West Midlands for deeper category and seasonal investigation because it ranks highest on both operational volume and population-adjusted burden.

## Crime is concentrated in a small number of categories

Violence and sexual offences account for approximately **699k records**, or around **44%** of total recorded crime.

Other high-volume categories include:

- Shoplifting: ~151k
- Criminal damage and arson: ~134k
- Vehicle crime: ~127k
- Public order: ~114k
- Other theft: ~111k

### Leadership action

Use category filtering before drawing conclusions from the overall headline. A large movement in a high-volume category can materially shift the total while lower-volume emerging risks can be hidden by the aggregate.

## Seasonal movement

The three-year pattern shows higher average monthly crime around late spring and summer.

**July is the highest average month**, while October is also repeatedly elevated.

### Leadership action

Use the investigation page to compare a current monthly increase against historic seasonal behaviour before deciding whether it represents an unusual exception.

## Recent category movement

2025/26 vs 2024/25:

- Drugs: **+15.3%**
- Possession of weapons: **+8.5%**
- Robbery: **+8.0%**
- Violence and sexual offences: **+3.8%**
- Shoplifting: **-5.0%**
- Burglary: **-5.0%**
- Vehicle crime: **-7.1%**

### Leadership action

Start with categories moving against the wider trend, then drill into force, month and seasonality.

## Further investigation priorities

### Staffordshire

Latest-year total increased **4.6%** vs 2024/25. Identify the categories and months driving the increase.

### Drugs, robbery and weapons

These categories increased year on year. Treat them as priorities for operational review, not evidence of a particular cause.

### West Midlands

Review the high population-adjusted rate alongside category mix, deprivation and officer-strength context.

### Seasonal readiness

Use historic monthly patterns ahead of higher-volume periods to identify whether current movement is expected or unusually high.
