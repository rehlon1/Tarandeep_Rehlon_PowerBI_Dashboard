# Executive Summary

The Police Crime Power BI Dashboard was developed as a decision-support tool for senior police leadership.

It converts three years of monthly recorded crime data into an interactive report covering **West Midlands Police, Staffordshire Police, West Mercia Police and Warwickshire Police**.

The dashboard supports three core activities:

- **Monitor** overall crime volume and movement over time
- **Compare** forces using both raw volume and population-adjusted rates
- **Investigate** crime categories, seasonal patterns and unusual changes

## Headline findings

- West Midlands has the highest total recorded crime volume at approximately **933k**
- West Midlands also has the highest average monthly crime rate at approximately **8.5 per 1,000 residents**
- Violence and sexual offences make up approximately **44%** of total recorded crime
- July has the highest average monthly volume across the three-year period
- In 2025/26, Drugs increased **15.3%**, Possession of weapons **8.5%**, and Robbery **8.0%** compared with 2024/25
- Staffordshire's latest-year total increased **4.6%** vs 2024/25

## Decision-support value

The dashboard is designed to move the user from a headline movement into a more focused question.

For example:

```text
Overall crime changed
        ↓
Which category drove it?
        ↓
Which force is affected?
        ↓
Which months are unusual?
        ↓
Is the movement seasonal or exceptional?
        ↓
What requires operational follow-up?
```

The report therefore supports prioritisation rather than claiming to establish the cause of recorded crime changes.
